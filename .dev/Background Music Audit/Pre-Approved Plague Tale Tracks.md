# Pre-Approved Tracks from "A Plague Tale"
I manually listened to the "A Plague Tale" OST a few years ago, when I used it for the prequel Chronicle to _Toronto Rising_, _Toronto By Night_.  I was able to recover the files, divided into categories:

- "Main" -- Tracks I found suitable for regular background music play regardless of scene. (I ended up using these tracks almost exclusively.)
- "Intrigue" -- Slightly more intense tracks originally intended for scenes of high tension or intrigue, but as I mentioned above, I rarely used this (mostly because it was a pain to remember to switch over)
- "Combat" -- Another notch up the intensity scale, intended for scenes of action or violence.

So these can be used to test the results of any processes we run on the other tracks: If they sort the tracks similarly, then we know we've configured everything properly.  We shouldn't use this as an absolute metric, though, because I'm sure my human ears and human survey of the tracks wasn't perfect!

**Note: The numbers are not track numbers from the OST, but rather numbers I applied after the fact. Track numbers followed by a letter indicate a portion of the track; other parts of the track may have been moved into other categories (e.g. part of "The Inquisition" was assigned to "Main", while another part was assigned to "Intrigue")**
